Good Intent & Public Purpose

This application has been developed in good intent and for general public utility purposes based on open health directory datasets made publicly available through the Open Government Data (data.gov.in) portal and the Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX / OpenStreetMap).

Ground Verification Advisory: Users are earnestly requested to verify facility availability, emergency readiness, and specific services with local health authorities or frontline healthcare workers — such as ASHA, ANM, Health Supervisors, BMOH (Block Medical Officer of Health) / Block Medical Officers, or the concerned healthcare facility directly for surety.

1. General Information Only

AarogyaMap India is a geospatial directory tool designed to help citizens and healthcare professionals locate healthcare infrastructure across India. The content provided on this application does not constitute professional medical advice, medical diagnosis, clinical opinion, or treatment recommendations.

2. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Use of this application, including viewing facility directories or routing directions, does not establish a doctor-patient relationship between the user and the developer, contributors, or listed health facilities.

3. Ground Reality and Operational Dynamics

Healthcare facilities operate dynamically. Bed capacity, operational status, physician attendance, availability of ICU/oxygen, and specialty departments are subject to change. Ground validation through local public health personnel (ASHA, ANM, BMOH) is always recommended.

4. Shortest Route & Directions

Shortest path and distance estimates are computed via geodesic algorithms (Haversine) and open-source routing (OSRM). Real-time road closures, construction, weather hazards, or traffic congestion may affect travel time.

100% Client-Side Privacy • Zero Telemetry • No Tracking

1. Zero Personal Health Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personal Health Information (PHI) or Personally Identifiable Information (PII). No registration, mobile number, or sign-in is required to access the full features of this app.

2. Device GPS / AGPS Location Handling

When you enable location permissions on your mobile or web browser, your latitude and longitude coordinates are processed exclusively inside your local browser memory to calculate straight-line distances and shortest path routes to nearby facilities. Your coordinates are never sent to any server, database, or analytics provider.

3. Offline Storage & Progressive Web App (PWA)

To enable resilient offline access during network blackouts, this application caches application shell assets and public directory datasets locally on your device via standard browser Service Workers and Cache API.

4. Third-Party Map & Routing Services

Base map tiles and geodesic routes are fetched from OpenStreetMap, Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team, Esri ArcGIS, and OSRM under standard non-tracking public API policies.
